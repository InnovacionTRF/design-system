Small pill for status/metadata. Tones map to the semantic palette.

```jsx
<Badge>borrador</Badge>
<Badge tone="orange">en revisión</Badge>
<Badge tone="success" dot>activo</Badge>
<Badge tone="info">programado</Badge>
<Badge tone="danger">detenido</Badge>
```

Tones: `neutral | orange | success | info | danger`. `dot` adds a leading status dot.
