Loading placeholder with a shimmer sweep. Stack a few at different widths to suggest text lines.

```jsx
<Skeleton width="75%" height={12} />
<Skeleton height={12} />
<Skeleton width="50%" height={12} />
```

Los keyframes vienen en `tokens/animation.css` vía `styles.css`: no hay que declarar nada en la página.

Props: `width`, `height`, `radius`.
